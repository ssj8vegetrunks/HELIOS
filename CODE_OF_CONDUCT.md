# HELIOS Code of Conduct

## Our standard

HELIOS should be a welcoming project for players, testers, translators, and
developers of every background and experience level.

Examples of welcome participation include:

- being patient and constructive when reporting or reproducing a problem;
- respecting different levels of technical knowledge;
- discussing ideas and code without attacking the person behind them;
- accepting corrections gracefully; and
- protecting private information found in logs, screenshots, or reports.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained personal attacks
are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in the HELIOS repository and in public spaces where a
person is representing the project, including issue discussions, pull requests,
and project testing threads.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner. Do not open a public issue if
the report contains private or sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical. The privacy
and safety of the reporter will be respected.
